Sources, model age & operating limits

    Historical point data: Jeff Sackmann's tennis datasets via a checksum-pinned public archive. Attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms are in the source notes. Upcoming matches: ESPN and Tennis.com; tournament metadata: Tennis.com; scoring rules: ATP/WTA.

    Updates are requested at minutes 17 and 47 each hour. GitHub may delay runs or disable schedules after 60 days of repository inactivity. Results stay visible after 90 minutes and after scheduled start, labeled as saved pre-match research. They are not live predictions or confirmed match outcomes. If a scan produces no picks, the last successful scan remains available with its original timestamp. Refresh reloads published results and keeps a validated copy on this device when browser storage is available. To request a new model run, open the workflow and use Run workflow while signed in to GitHub. Tournament metadata is discovered and refreshed automatically each scan. Unknown surfaces, unsupported scoring formats and conflicting fixtures are withheld.

    Only sanitized source, text parameters and public tennis data are in the repository. The site receives a small results file. No model binaries, accounts, analytics, credentials, paid services or card setup.
