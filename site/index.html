Sources, model age & operating limits

    Historical point data: Jeff Sackmann's tennis datasets via a checksum-pinned public archive. Attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms are in the source notes. Upcoming matches: ESPN; tournament surfaces and formats: ATP/WTA.

    Hourly updates are requested at minute 17. GitHub may delay runs or disable schedules after 60 days of repository inactivity. Forecasts disappear when a snapshot is 90 minutes old or a match reaches its scheduled start. New tournaments need verified surface and format entries; unknown events are withheld.

    Only sanitized source, text parameters and public tennis data are in the repository. The site receives a small results file. No model binaries, accounts, analytics, credentials, paid services or card setup.
